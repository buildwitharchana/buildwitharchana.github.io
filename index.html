Pay in 3 — High-Impact Merchant Incident

A production incident demonstrating ownership, technical troubleshooting, customer communication and rapid mitigation.

Restoring a critical payment flow under pressure

Situation

A high-volume enterprise merchant experienced transaction failures affecting the Pay in 3 payment option.

Ownership

As the technical lead, I took ownership of the incident, coordinated the team and focused first on minimizing merchant and customer impact.

Immediate Mitigation

We temporarily disabled the affected payment option through merchant configuration while keeping other payment methods available.

Investigation

I worked with the team to troubleshoot the payment flow, isolate the failure and identify the issue in the merchant-side implementation.

Resolution

I coordinated the technical fix, validated the payment flow and monitored the system after the production deployment.

Aftercare

I supported RCA and follow-up actions focused on monitoring, alerts and preventive measures to reduce the likelihood of recurrence.
